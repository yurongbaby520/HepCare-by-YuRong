Q1. 化學治療可能會降低免疫力，增加 B 型肝炎病毒再活化的風險。

Chemotherapy may weaken the immune system and increase the risk of hepatitis B virus (HBV) reactivation.

Q2. 化療前接受 B 型肝炎預防性治療，有助於預防 B 型肝炎病毒再活化。

Receiving preventive antiviral therapy before chemotherapy helps reduce the risk of hepatitis B virus (HBV) reactivation.

Q3. 即使肝指數正常，仍應依照醫囑持續服用 B 型肝炎抗病毒藥。

Even if your liver enzyme levels are normal, you should continue taking hepatitis B antiviral medication as prescribed by your doctor.

Q4. 遵從醫囑、規律服藥及定期追蹤，有助於預防 B 型肝炎病毒再活化。

Following your doctor's instructions, taking your medication as prescribed, and attending regular follow-up visits help prevent HBV reactivation.
